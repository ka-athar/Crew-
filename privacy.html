Privacy policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

Crew Ledger is a private workspace used to assign work and track deadlines. It is run by your workspace administrator. By checking the consent box at sign-in, you confirm that you have read this policy. You can open it in a separate tab before accepting.

What is stored

  • Your account: full name, username, email address and a one-way scrambled form of your password. The password itself is never stored and cannot be read by anyone, including your team lead.
  • Tasks assigned to you: title, details, start date, deadline, priority, workflow status, task type, estimate, list/stage, sprint/cycle and tags. Workflow statuses include To do, In progress, Submitted, Under review, Needs changes, Waiting teacher’s approval and Completed.
  • An activity record of task assignments and status changes, including who acted and when.
  • Attendance records: one daily record per person, with server-recorded time-in, time-out, date, workspace, session duration and estimated active-app time based on visible-page heartbeats. Time-in is created automatically immediately after successful sign-in for the day; sign-out or a browser exit attempts to record time-out. Active-app time is an estimate based on periodic heartbeats while the app tab is visible, not a guarantee that a person is actively working. If a browser or device is force-stopped before the exit request is sent, the app can only reconcile the record when it reconnects, using the last server heartbeat.
  • Files you deliberately upload, including the filename, file type, approximate size, uploader and upload time. Uploads are limited to 4 MB per file and are stored with workspace data until removed by the workspace operator.
  • Workspace names, workspace membership and task-to-workspace assignments.
  • Workspace chat: channel and group names, message text, sender and message time. Each workspace has separate channels and group conversations. Workspace-wide channels can be read by that workspace’s active members and lead; private group conversations can be read only by the selected group members and the primary super-admin.
  • A sign-in session token on each device you use for up to 14 days. The app still requires local device verification on every launch/restart and when returning from the background.
  • A timestamp and version number when you accept this Privacy Policy at sign-in.
  • If you choose to enable device passkeys for attendance, the server stores the public-key credential and its counter. The device performs user verification locally using a fingerprint, face recognition or device PIN; Crew Ledger does not receive or store biometric images or fingerprint templates.

What is not collected

  • Location, contacts, microphone and camera data are not collected automatically. Photos and documents are accessed only when you choose them in the upload picker; selected files are uploaded to the app server for work purposes.
  • Advertising or analytics trackers. The app sets no tracking cookies.

Who can see it

  • Your workspace lead can see accounts, tasks, attendance, reports and activity for their own workspace. The primary super-admin can oversee all four workspaces.
  • Team members can see their own profile and tasks, workspace-shared uploads, their own attendance history, and messages/channels available inside their workspace.
  • The primary super-admin can oversee accounts, tasks, reports, attendance, activity and files across all workspaces.
  • The app is hosted on Netlify, which stores its data. Whoever manages the Netlify account for this app can technically reach that data.

Third-party fonts

To display text in its intended typefaces, the app loads font files from Google Fonts. That request tells Google your device's address. Your team lead can switch to self-hosted fonts to avoid this.

How the app is protected

  • Passwords are hashed with scrypt. Temporary passwords set by the team lead must be replaced at first sign in.
  • Repeated wrong passwords lock sign in for 15 minutes.
  • Sign-in cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict, and carry the Secure flag over HTTPS.
  • Every request is checked on the server for the signed-in person's role. Members cannot read or change other people's private tasks. Channels and files are restricted to the matching workspace. Private group messages are restricted to selected group members as well; the primary administrator can oversee all workspaces.
  • Text is always displayed as plain text, and a strict content policy blocks injected scripts.
  • Deactivating an account signs that person out immediately.

Your choices

  • Change your password any time from More.
  • Ask your team lead to correct your account, review your records, or request export/deletion. The workspace operator should establish and follow a retention schedule for attendance and uploads.
  • Sign out on shared devices from More.

Uploads and automatic assignment

Choosing a file from your phone may open the system photo library or file picker. A direct Google Drive account connection is not included; if your device exposes Drive through its file picker, you may be able to choose a file there. The automatic assignment helper currently parses text/CSV instructions in the format “Member Name: task description — YYYY-MM-DD”. It does not understand every PDF, Word document or image, and every generated assignment should be reviewed by a team lead before being relied upon.

Questions
